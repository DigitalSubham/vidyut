# Patna Go-To-Market Plan

**Prepared:** July 2026 · Companion to `BIHAR_MARKET_FEASIBILITY.md`
Covers: sales process, first 10 / 25 / 100 customers, channels, partnerships, pilot, objections, trust-building, seasonality, field routing, launch phases.

---

## 1. GTM Thesis

Win Patna with **founder-led, outcome-based direct selling** to owner-run English-medium private schools (200–800 students), using **free data migration + a paid pilot + local support** as the wedge. Convert pilots to annual contracts, harvest **testimonials and referrals**, then expand area-by-area. Do not scale channels until the pilot cohort proves adoption and renewal. **The first 25 customers are a validation and reference-building exercise, not a revenue target.**

## 2. The Sales Process (13 steps)

| # | Step | What good looks like | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Lead identification** | Census + directory frame; A/B/C scored (see `PATNA_SCHOOL_PROSPECTS.md`) | Founder |
| 2 | **First contact** | Warm intro > referral > relevant in-person walk-in > phone. No spam blasts. | Founder |
| 3 | **School visit** | Meet the **owner/director** (decision-maker), not just clerk. Discovery, not pitch. | Founder |
| 4 | **Product demonstration** | 15–20 min, outcome-led (fees→receipt→parent alert→report card). Their data if possible. | Founder |
| 5 | **Trial / pilot offer** | Time-boxed (4–6 wks), discounted, with a defined conversion date | Founder |
| 6 | **Data collection** | Get their Excel/registers; sign a simple data + pilot agreement | Founder |
| 7 | **Data migration** | Done-for-you import; clean duplicates; opening fee balances | Founder/onboarding |
| 8 | **Staff training** | 1 onsite session for clerk + 2–3 teachers; leave Hindi video links | Founder/onboarding |
| 9 | **Activation / go-live** | Go-live checklist met: fees configured, attendance live, parent app invites sent | Founder |
| 10 | **Payment** | Convert at agreed date; annual invoice + setup fee; timed to admission-season cash | Founder |
| 11 | **Support** | WhatsApp-first; weekly check-in during first 30 days | Support |
| 12 | **Renewal** | Annual, pre-empted 45 days out; show ROI (collection %, defaulters down) | Founder/CS |
| 13 | **Referral request** | Ask every happy owner for 2 intros; offer referral incentive | Founder |

**Best time of day to visit:** mid-morning (after assembly/prayer, ~10:30–12:30) or after school (~2:30–4:00), avoiding arrival/dispersal chaos. `[Inferred]`
**Best time of year:** see §8 seasonality — **Nov–Apr** is prime (pre-session planning + admission + new-session setup). Avoid May–June (holidays) and peak board-exam weeks (Feb–Mar) for demos, though admission-office staff are reachable Mar–Apr.
**Visits to close:** 2–4 for a motivated owner in-season; 5+ or stalled for committee/premium schools.
**Follow-up cadence:** within 48 hrs of each visit; then weekly; stop-loss after 4 unproductive touches (park as "revisit next season").

## 3. First 10 Customers (Phase 0 → Phase 1)

**Goal:** prove pain, onboarding, adoption, and willingness to pay — not revenue.

- Source from **warm intros** (1–2 friendly principals/owners the founder can reach) + top A-tier census names.
- Offer a **paid pilot at 40–50% off Year 1** (paid, not free — free pilots don't test WTP and don't get taken seriously).
- Hand-hold every one: free migration, onsite go-live, weekly review.
- Target mix: mostly Standard-fit (200–800 students), 1–2 smaller Starter-fit to test that tier.
- Success = 5–10 live, teachers marking attendance daily by day 30, ≥60% intend to renew at full price, ≥1 testimonial each, ≥2 referrals total.
- **Timeline:** ~2–3 months of intense founder effort.

## 4. First 25 Customers (Phase 2 — Local Credibility)

- Convert pilot referrals + expand across A-tier areas (Kankarbagh → Boring Road → Bailey Road → Ashiana → Rajapur).
- Introduce **2 published case studies** (e.g., "School X cut fee-collection time 60%, recovered ₹Y in dues") and a **referral program** (₹ credit or free months for both parties).
- Begin light **partnership** conversations (CAs, computer/biometric dealers) — see §6.
- Still founder-led; consider first **local sales/support hire** near school #20.
- Success = 25 paying (most at/near full price), <15% churn, repeatable 3–8 week cycle, support <2 hrs/school/week.
- **Timeline:** months ~4–12.

## 5. First 100 Customers (Phase 3 — Patna Expansion)

- Systematic area-by-area coverage of all A/B Patna clusters + Danapur–Khagaul–Phulwari belt.
- Team: founder + 1–2 field reps + 1 dedicated support/onboarding person.
- Channels beyond direct: **referrals (primary), partnerships (CAs/dealers/consultants), targeted local digital (Google/Facebook to Patna school owners), a demo seminar/principal meet.**
- Introduce **online fee payment** (adds revenue + stickiness).
- Success = 100 paying, operating break-even in sight (~100–140 schools), <12% churn, NPS/referral engine running.
- **Timeline:** months ~12–30 (Base case).

## 8. Seasonality — 12-Month Sales Calendar `[Verified: CBSE session Apr–Mar; Inferred activity mapping]`

CBSE/most-school session runs **April 1 → March 31**; admissions Jan–Apr; fees peak at session start + quarterly.

| Month | School context | Best activity |
|---|---|---|
| **Apr** | New session starts; admissions; fee collection peak | **Onboard/go-live** (fresh session = clean cutover); close deals |
| **May** | Summer holidays begin | Off-site build/onboarding backlog; light selling |
| **Jun** | Holidays / reopening | Prep; partner outreach; content |
| **Jul** | Session settled; Q1 fees | **Discovery + demos**; sell "fix this before next term" |
| **Aug–Sep** | Mid-term; half-yearly prep | Demos; sell **exam/report-card** value ahead of exams |
| **Oct** | Half-yearly exams; festivals (Durga Puja/Diwali/Chhath) | Low activity around festivals; nurture |
| **Nov** | Post-festival; planning next session | **Prime selling** — owners plan systems/budgets |

## 10. Demo Strategy

**Duration:** 15–20 min max. Outcome-led, in **Hindi/Hinglish**, on **mobile + web**, using *their* sample data where possible.

**Flow (refined for the Patna owner):**
1. Import a class of students (show it's their data, fast).
2. Collect a fee → **print/share a receipt instantly** (the "wow" for owner + clerk).
3. Show **fee-dues + defaulter list** (owner's cash pain).
4. Mark attendance on the teacher app → **parent gets an absence alert** (show the parent phone).
5. Enter marks → **generate a report card** (clerk's term pain gone).
6. Owner dashboard: collection %, dues, attendance at a glance.

**What to avoid:** feature tours of 25 modules; technical/architecture talk; English-only; long slide decks; over-promising unbuilt features.
**Close with:** "Which of these — fees, parent alerts, or report cards — costs your team the most time today?" then propose the paid pilot with a go-live date.

## 11. Objection-Handling Guide

For each: underlying concern → response → proof required → when *not* to push.

| Objection | Real concern | Response | Proof | When not to push |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| "We already use registers" | Change risk, effort | We keep it simple; free migration; run parallel one term | Migration demo, testimonial | If owner sees zero pain — requalify |
| "Teachers won't use it" | Adoption fear | Teacher app is 2 taps; we train onsite; adoption tracked | Live teacher app, adoption stats | If leadership won't mandate use |
| "Parents don't need an app" | Value doubt | Start with SMS; app is opt-in; absence/fee alerts reduce owner's calls | Parent-alert demo | If truly no smartphone parents |
| "Can't afford it" | Budget/ROI | It's ~0.5% of fee revenue; recovers more in dues than it costs; annual = 1 admission's fees | ROI math, dues-recovery case | If school is financially distressed |
| "Internet is unreliable" | Usability | Offline attendance/marks; SMS fallback; lightweight app | Offline demo | If area truly has no data at all |
| "What if you shut down?" | Vendor risk | Written SLA + **guaranteed data export** anytime; local entity | Contract clause, export button | Never dismiss — answer fully |
| "Our data may be stolen" | Trust/security | Encrypted, backed up, access-controlled; you own & can export | Security one-pager | Never dismiss |
| "Another vendor is cheaper" | Price | Cheaper apps fail on reliability/support; show our SLA + references | Reference calls, uptime | If they only want lowest price — walk |
| "We already have software" | Switching | Free migration; what does yours *not* do? (usually mobile/support) | Gap demo | If deeply satisfied on national ERP |
| "We only need fees" | Narrow need | Start with fees module; grow later; still cheaper than point apps + better | Fees-only package | Fine — sell Starter/fees, land & expand |
| "We need customization" | Fit | Configurable templates (report cards, receipts) without custom code | Template config demo | If they demand bespoke dev — decline |
| "We don't want monthly payments" | Cash/commitment | Annual billing, timed to admission cash | Annual invoice option | Good — annual is our preference anyway |
| "We need Hindi support" | Language | UI + support in Hindi/Hinglish | Hindi UI live | Never a blocker for us — it's a strength |
| "We need onsite support" | Reliability | Onsite go-live included (Standard); ongoing WhatsApp/phone; onsite in Pro | Support tiers | If they demand unlimited free onsite at Starter price — reprice to Pro |
| "We tried ERP before and it failed" | Burned before | Ask why it failed (usually adoption/support); our onboarding fixes that; paid pilot de-risks | Pilot + adoption plan | Acknowledge, don't oversell |

## 12. Trust-Building Strategy (ranked)

Most important signals first (small schools buy on trust, not features):

1. **Free, done-for-you data migration** — removes risk + effort; proves competence.
2. **Local presence + local-language support** — "we're in Patna, call us."
3. **Written service agreement + guaranteed data export** — answers "what if you vanish."
4. **Reference schools / testimonials / case studies** — social proof from peers.
5. **Paid pilot with clear success criteria** — de-risks the decision.
6. **Data backup + security one-pager** — addresses theft/loss fears.
7. **Transparent pricing** (no hidden charges) — differentiates from "hidden ERP charges" reputation.
8. **Onsite go-live training** — hand-holding at the scary moment.
9. **Responsive WhatsApp support line** — the channel they already trust.
10. **Public roadmap / demo environment** — shows the product is real and evolving.

## 13. Pilot Program Design (3–10 schools)

- **Selection:** A-tier, size 200–800, level 1–3 digital, owner-committed, ideally 1–2 with visible fee pain (best case-study material). Mix in 1 Starter-size to test that tier.
- **Duration:** 4–6 weeks to go-live + 4 weeks observation (not open-ended).
- **Commercials:** **paid, discounted 40–50% Yr 1** (never free — free pilots don't test WTP or seriousness). Setup fee waived or halved.
- **Included:** MSP modules, free migration, onsite training, weekly review.
- **Success metrics:** go-live in ≤2 weeks; teacher attendance daily by day 30 (>70%); parent app active >50%; fee module in real use; owner reports time saved; renewal intent >80%; ≥2 referrals across cohort.
- **Boundaries:** defined support hours; no bespoke development; features list fixed for pilot.
- **Conversion:** explicit paid-conversion date; testimonial + referral request on success.
- **Contract:** simple 1–2 page pilot agreement (scope, data ownership, support hours, conversion terms).
