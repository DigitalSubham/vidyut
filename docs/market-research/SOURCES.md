## Evidence-quality reminder

- **Strongest (Tier 1–2):** Bihar/India school counts, management split, digital infra, academic calendar (Sources #1–7, #26, #29).
- **Directional (Tier 5–6):** competitor pricing ranges, connectivity/UPI, low-fee economics — confirm competitor prices via ethical quote requests (`[Field]`).
- **Leads only (Tier 8–9):** directory school lists and vendor blogs — never treated as authoritative; contact data to be gathered ethically and directly.
- **Not sourced (analytical):** all `[Estimated]`/`[Inferred]`/`[Field]` figures — TAM/SAM/SOM splits, WTP at price points, unit economics, city scores, forecasts. These are reasoned planning inputs, **not evidence**, and are the agenda for the Phase-0 field validation.
